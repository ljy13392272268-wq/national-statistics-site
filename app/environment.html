Environment

Less carbon per person, cleaner air

CO2 per person fell from 16.0 tonnes in 2015 to 13.6 tonnes in 2024, and average PM2.5 exposure fell from 8.1 to 7.0 micrograms per cubic metre by 2023. Forest cover barely moved, at 33.8% of the land, while renewables reached 10.9% of final energy use in 2021, the latest year reported.

Source: World Bank Open Data, 2015–2024.

Carbon per person is falling

CO2 emissions per capita, tonnes CO2e, 2015–2024. From 16.0 tonnes to 13.6 tonnes — down about 15% in nine years, with the sharpest drop in 2020.

Source: World Bank Open Data, indicator EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5, 2015–2024.

The air got cleaner

Mean annual PM2.5 exposure, 2015–2023 (2024 not yet reported). It bounced between 7.0 and 8.2 micrograms per cubic metre all decade and stood at 7.0 in 2023.

Source: World Bank Open Data, indicator EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3, 2015–2023.

Forest flat, renewables rising

Forest area barely moved, from 33.9% of the land in 2015 to 33.8% in 2024. Renewable energy rose from 8.9% of final energy use to 10.9% in 2021, the latest year the source reports.
