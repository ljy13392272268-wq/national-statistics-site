Education

Almost everyone goes to school — fewer go on to university

Secondary enrolment was 97.5% in 2022, the latest year reported, and primary enrolment 97.0%. Tertiary enrolment fell from 88.9% in 2015 to 79.4% in 2022. Government spending on education was 5.4% of GDP in 2021, and classes averaged about 14 pupils per teacher in 2017, the last year the source reports.

Source: World Bank Open Data; enrolment last reported for 2022, spending for 2021, pupil–teacher ratios for 2017.

The university slide

Gross tertiary enrolment, 2015–2022: from 88.9% to 79.4%. The ratio counts all students enrolled against the population of the typical university age, so it can exceed the share of one cohort.

Source: World Bank Open Data, indicator SE.TER.ENRR, 2015–2022.

Nearly every child in school

Gross enrolment in primary and secondary school, 2015–2022. Both stayed near or above 97%, with secondary peaking above 100% in 2021 — gross ratios count repeaters and over-age pupils, so they can pass 100%.

Source: World Bank Open Data, indicators SE.PRM.ENRR and SE.SEC.ENRR, 2015–2022.
