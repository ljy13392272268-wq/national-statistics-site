About the data

National series

World Bank Open Data, country USA, 2015–2024. The latest year differs by series: enrolment and education spending stop in 2021–2022, health spending in 2023, PM2.5 in 2023. A dash means the source reports no value — not zero.

State figures

GDP: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, 2024. Population: 2020 Decennial Census, U.S. Census Bureau. GDP per person divides 2024 GDP by 2020 population, so treat it as indicative.

Display and sources

Values are rounded for display only; the CSV download carries the full figures. Every chart names its indicator code and years beneath it.
